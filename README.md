# GEOG5990

Do health promoting neighbourhoods have loewr neighbourhood churn in Manchester?

Background and context:


Data used:
CDRC Residential Mobility Index
- the proportion of households that have changed between a year (going back to 1997) and the beginning of 2023
- it is useful in this analysis for representing the neighbourhood 'churn' as it is represented as small geographical scale (LSOAs) and it allows for more short term analysis (over 5 years) as opposed to relying on census data which is collected every 10 years
- it is estimated based on administrative and consumer datasets including electoral registers, consumer registers and land registry house sale data
- data available at LAD (2021 boundaries) and LSOA (2011 boundaries) level.
- for this analysis, the LSOA (2011) boundaries are used due to smaller scale. Therefore 2011 LSOA data was downloaded as well as 2021 to enable joining of datasets.
- recent RMI records should be interpreted with caution due to delays in registered address changes


CDRC Access to Health Assets and Hazards (AHAH)(Version 4):
- multi-dimensional index representing how 'healthy' neighbourhoods are in terms of access to certain retail environments, health services and physical environment (green and blue spaces and air quality)
- it consists of a total of 14 inputs, making up 4 domains
- high quality and low bias data sources
